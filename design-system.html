05 — Image presentation rules

Always

  • Use an <img> at its native aspect ratio (height:auto)
  • Put text beside or below the photo
  • Use a light-blue mat offset for a premium framed look
  • Show product detail shots in a contain-box with a neutral background

Never

  • Use a photo as a background image with a text overlay
  • object-fit: cover on a phone or van photo
  • Fix a height that crops the bottom (the phone) or the centre (the van logo)
  • Stretch a photo beyond ~1.5× its 800 px source

06 — Spacing and layout

Section padding
Block gap (64)
Heading gap (48)
Element gap (16)
Side gutter

Container 1240 px. Section padding scales from 64 px (mobile) to 120 px (desktop), and the side gutter from 16 to 40 px. Sections alternate white and surface; navy bands are used sparingly (VoIP benefits, emergency CTA). Breakpoints: 576 / 768 / 992 / 1200. These match Elementor's mobile, tablet, laptop and desktop presets.

09 — Desktop vs mobile direction

Desktop (≥1200)

Top bar with phones and 24/7 badge · sticky white header with full nav and hover dropdowns · split sections alternating left/right · photos ~55 % of the row · 3-column feature rows.

Mobile (≤575)

Top bar keeps the badge and phone numbers (tap to call) · hamburger opens a full-width drawer with tap-to-expand submenus · photo first, full width, uncropped · CTA buttons go full width.
