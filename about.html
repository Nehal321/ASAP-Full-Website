ASAP company profile

Our Story

After many years in the Telecommunications industry, the founders of ASAP Business Communications chose Antelope Valley for its Corporate headquarters in 2001. Although our customer base extends throughout California and beyond, it is our dedication to all our customers that make us unique.

ASAP Business Communications provides a one stop vendor solution for all of your office voice, internet and telecom needs thanks to the convergence of voice and data. We are in the business of providing Traditional Telephones and Hosted PBX as well as voice and data network carrier solutions over a reliable network that is proven to be the best solution to connect your business to the rest of the world.

Although we can save you money because rates have dropped, it is not just about saving money anymore. The days of having multiple telecom vendors and several phone bills are over.

Our company philosophy

Treat Every Customer Like Our Best Friend

With an emphasis on customer service, our technical staff is well trained in all aspects of business telephone systems. Our premise is simple: treat each customer as if he/she were our best friend and always leave the customer with the feeling that he/she received more than what was expected. Our goal is to provide a level of service that is unsurpassed.

An atmosphere of “whatever the customer wants, we will make it happen” is how we approach each relationship with our customers, whether long time customers or new to the ASAP family.
